# Qualification audio

Google FLEURS contributors; AMI/AMIDA consortium. CC BY 4.0. Exact URLs, byte ranges and SHA-256 in manifest.json. FLEURS files are unmodified archive members; AMI files are continuous crops with unchanged PCM samples. See ../human/ATTRIBUTION.md and ../human/AMI-LICENCE.txt. No TTS or assembled speech. Two new read-speech controls were selected after freeze-current.json and before inference; two known spontaneous repairs are regression tests, not independent validation. Model-training overlap with public corpora is unknown.
